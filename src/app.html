    Radio settings

    If you press the button, you'll be told when to hold it and when to let go.
    Longer gives voice-activated transmit time to switch on.
    Too loud distorts the tones. Start around half and adjust if the other end sees damaged packets.
    SpeedChoose 1200 or 300 baud in the top bar. 300 is four times slower but survives echo, so use it through a speaker and microphone, in echoey rooms or on weak signals. Listening always hears both speeds.

    About Smoke Signal

    A modem in your browser. It turns messages, photos and files into sound that any radio can carry, and turns that sound back into messages. It works offline, and nothing you send or receive leaves this device except as sound.

    How it works

    Signal
    Bell 202 AFSK tones at 1200 and 2200 Hz, at 1200 or 300 baud
    Packets
    AX.25 UI frames with a CRC, the same format as APRS. Direwolf can decode them.
    Files
    Split into blocks with extra recovery packets, so a file arrives even when some packets are lost. Resends join up with what was heard before.
    Replies
    A station a message is addressed to replies with a short acknowledgement, so the sender stops resending.

    Before you transmit: in the UK, sending data on amateur radio bands needs a licence and your callsign, which is sent with every packet. Messages must not be encrypted. Leaving it repeating while you're away from the radio counts as unattended operation, which has its own conditions. Licence-free radios have their own rules. Check with Ofcom before using this on air.
